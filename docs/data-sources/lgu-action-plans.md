# LGU action plans

After each audit, the audited government writes an action plan for COA's recommendations,
and COA later checks what was done. Some governments post these on their own websites,
which aren't behind COA's bot check.

## Maramag, CY 2023

- File: [Action-Plan-Monitoring-Tool-Maramag-2023_APMT.pdf](https://www.maramag.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Action-Plan-Monitoring-Tool-Maramag-2023_APMT.pdf)
  (35 pages, 424 KB, text can be extracted)
- Title: "Action Plan Monitoring Tool", Annex B
- Sector: Local Government Audit Sector E, team R10-01
- Audit period: Calendar Year 2023. AAR date: April 8, 2024
- Prepared October 17, 2024; reviewed and approved October 31, 2024

### Layout

Two sections:

1. **Current year audit findings and recommendations, as in Part II of the AAR for CY 2023.**
   16 numbered findings.
2. **Prior years' audit findings and recommendations, as in Part III of the AAR for CY 2023.**

Each row has two halves:

- **Agency action plan and status of implementation**: audit observation, recommendation,
  action, person or department responsible, target dates, the town's status, reason for
  partial or no implementation.
- **Results of COA validation**: date of follow-up, COA's status of implementation, actual
  implementation dates, remarks.

Statuses used: Fully Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented. Across both
halves the text has 28 "Fully", 1 "Partially" and 3 "Not Implemented". Per-finding counts
still need a careful read, because the PDF's columns run together when extracted.

Examples of findings (paraphrased here, quote them from the AAR on the site):

- Two completed infrastructure projects turned over to barangays were removed from the
  Trust Fund books without deeds of transfer or certificates of turnover and acceptance.
- Unpaid financial assistance to various barangays.
- The grant of the Personnel Motivation Incentive (PMI).
- A prior-year item on refunding unutilized local government funds to the Bureau of the
  Treasury.

### What this confirms

- AAR Part II holds the year's findings and Part III the prior years', as the plan assumes.
- COA itself checks and records whether each recommendation was carried out. The site can
  show that check, labeled with its date ([data rule 2](../data-rules.md)).

### Caveat

The document names the COA staff who prepared, reviewed and approved it. The site doesn't
show those names ([data rule 11](../data-rules.md)).

## Other governments

COA's own report ZIPs carry these documents from CY 2023 on. Every CY 2023 and CY 2024 ZIP
has an `AAPSI_APMT` folder with `<Name><year>_AAPSI.pdf` (Agency Action Plan and Status of
Implementation) and `<Name><year>_APMT.pdf` (Action Plan Monitoring Tool), for all 23
governments. Most block copying, like the executive summaries. The CY 2022 ZIPs hold only
the report. We haven't read them yet, so it's still open
whether they match Maramag's layout above and which audit year's plan they hold
([coa-annual-audit-reports.md](coa-annual-audit-reports.md#what-a-report-looks-like)).

LGU websites are a fallback when COA's site is down: look for "Action Plan Monitoring
Tool", "AAPSI" or "audit".
